About

A little about me

Medicine, translational science, oncology research, education, and projects that turn useful ideas into something tangible.

I’m currently pursuing my medical education at Ohio University Heritage College of Osteopathic Medicine in Columbus, Ohio. I earned a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from Wright State University and later completed a Master of Science in Translational Pharmacology at The Ohio State University before beginning medical school.

My primary research interests are in genitourinary oncology, with a particular focus on bladder and prostate cancer. I work with Dr. Katharine Collier, a genitourinary medical oncologist at The Ohio State University, on research involving GU cancers.

I also have experience working on clinical trials in prostate, bladder, kidney, and testicular cancers. Those experiences have helped shape my interest in clinically relevant research and translating new evidence into better patient care.

Alongside medicine and research, I enjoy building educational tools. Drug of the Day is my iPhone app designed to help healthcare learners review one medication at a time.

Additional professional information and updates are available on my LinkedIn profile.
